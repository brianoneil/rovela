How a trip is told

After the cover, four ways back into the same eight days.

  1. Map

    Days in color, photos on the track, the whole journey in one view.

  2. Chronicle

    The days in order: walks, places, pictures, and the notes you kept.

  3. Day

    Weather, elevation, and the hours of a single date.

  4. Story

    A chapter you can read, edit, and keep.

What it keeps

The route as you moved, the pictures you took, and the few things you chose to write down. After the trip, they are one story.
